Mahkus' solar + Powerwall home, by the numbers

If you've been following along with my YouTube journey with our system, I thought it'd be a good time to put up a bunch of data to help show you the benefits of solar and Powerwall. This home in the Sacramento suburbs has 12.24 kW of solar panels on an east-facing roof and three Tesla Powerwall 3 batteries. The system was installed in August 2021 with three Powerwall 2s, which were recalled and replaced with Powerwall 3s in July 2026. The home is on PG&E's NEM 2 net-metering program with the E-TOU-D time-of-use rate, gets its electricity generation from Valley Clean Energy, and is enrolled in a Virtual Power Plant program that sends battery power to the grid during emergencies. This page shows how much the panels produce, what the electricity really costs after the yearly true-ups, how much the system has saved and when it pays for itself, and what the Virtual Power Plant events earn.
